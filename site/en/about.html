About TOPDA

What this site covers

Practical information for buying, leasing, financing, taxing, moving into, and renovating real estate in Korea, plus auction procedures. Everything is based on primary sources: the Civil Code, Local Tax Act, Income Tax Act, Housing Lease Protection Act, official Ministry guidance, and court rulings. All calculators and checklists run entirely in your browser; no data is sent or stored on any server.
