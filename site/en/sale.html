Buying & selling guide

Buying & Selling

A practical path from setting your budget and inspecting a property through signing, closing, registration, and taxes.

View the full timeline

What to check at each stage

The details that prevent expensive surprises
Property viewing

Evaluate the home, not just the listing

Check daylight, orientation, layout, ventilation, moisture, noise, water pressure, parking, building management, nearby schools and transit. Revisit at a different time of day before deciding.

Read the detailed guide → Legal rights

Read the property register

Confirm the registered owner and review ownership restrictions, mortgages, provisional seizures, trusts, and other encumbrances. Obtain a fresh copy before the deposit and again before closing.

Read the detailed guide → Contract

Put important promises in writing

Record the condition of the property, included fixtures, mortgage cancellation, vacancy and handover, unapproved alterations, closing adjustments, breach remedies, and the documents due at closing.

Read the detailed guide → Closing

Coordinate payment and registration

Recheck the register, verify identity and authority, transfer the balance only when the required documents are ready, settle prorated fees, record meter readings, and submit title registration without delay.

Read the detailed guide → Taxes

Budget beyond the purchase price

Allow for acquisition tax, local education tax, possible rural special tax, brokerage commission, legal or registration fees, housing bonds, moving costs, and financing expenses.
