Total Cost Dashboard

All the numbers a Korean real-estate deal needs, in one place — taxes, loans, and closing costs for buying, selling, leasing, inheriting, or receiving a gift.

Purchase details

Non-housing (retail/officetel/land) also has a mandatory National Housing Bond purchase at registration, and a flat 4.6% acquisition tax. Loans are reviewed under DSR for household loans (non-housing collateral is assumed to amortize over 8 years) or under RTI for a registered rental-business loan.

Mortgage

Equal principal has a larger first payment, so DSR is assessed higher (more conservative).
Rental-business loans are assessed by RTI, not DSR. RTI is calculated together using the loan amount/rate above.

Registration & scrivener

Leave blank if unsure → estimated as 70% of purchase price. Check the exact assessed value via the icon above.

Sale details

The sale scenario focuses on capital gains tax, so income/DSR are not applied.

Jeonse contract

Jeonse loans guaranteed by HUG/HF/SGI are excluded from household DSR (per the FSS's household-loan management guidance). Some "Jeonse loan + credit top-up" products still affect your separate credit-loan limit — check with your bank.

Inheritance details

KRW 50M personal deduction per child (minors get an extra KRW 10M × remaining years to age 19).

Gift details

10-year cumulative deduction: spouse KRW 600M / adult child KRW 50M / minor KRW 20M / other relative KRW 10M.

Income · DSR check

For DSR, a credit loan is annualized as balance ÷ 5 years.
