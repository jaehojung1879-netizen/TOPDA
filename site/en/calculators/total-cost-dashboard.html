Total Cost Dashboard

Taxes, loan limits, and closing costs for a Korean property deal, calculated together — buying, selling, leasing costs for buying, selling, leasing, inheriting, or receiving a gift.

Purchase details

Non-housing (retail/officetel/land) also has a mandatory National Housing Bond purchase at registration, and a flat 4.6% acquisition tax. Loans are reviewed under DSR for household loans (non-housing collateral is assumed to amortize over 8 years) or under RTI for a registered rental-business loan.

Relief eligibility is checked automatically from price, home count, and floor area.

Final relief = statutory 25% + ordinance 0–25%. Not a flat 50% nationwide.

Mortgage

Equal principal has a larger first payment, so DSR is assessed higher (more conservative).
Rental-business loans are assessed by RTI, not DSR. RTI is calculated together using the loan amount/rate above.

Sale details

For joint ownership, the gain is split equally and each owner's tax is calculated before summing the total.

The sale scenario focuses on capital gains tax, so income/DSR are not applied.

Surcharge waiver — transitional rule

A transfer after 9 May 2026 still qualifies if the contract was signed and the down payment received by that date, and the transfer occurs within 4 months (6 months in newly designated regulated areas).

Jeonse contract

Jeonse loans guaranteed by HUG/HF/SGI are excluded from household DSR (per the FSS's household-loan management guidance). Some "Jeonse loan + credit top-up" products still affect your separate credit-loan limit — check with your bank.

Inheritance details

KRW 50M personal deduction per child (minors get an extra KRW 10M × remaining years to age 19).
Leave blank to see a range between the KRW 500M minimum deduction and the statutory-share ceiling.

Gift details

10-year cumulative deduction: spouse KRW 600M / adult child KRW 50M / minor KRW 20M / other relative KRW 10M (within 6 degrees of blood or 4 of affinity). Gifts between unrelated parties get no deduction.
