Acquisition Tax Calculator

Purchase · inheritance · gift · new build · automatic relief and surcharge checks
Use the contract price for a purchase, or the recognized assessed value for a gift or inheritance.
A newly built apartment bought from a developer is a paid transfer (1–3%, or the heavy rate), not an original acquisition at 2.8%. Original acquisition applies only when the owner built or extended the structure themselves. Choosing the wrong one changes the tax significantly.
