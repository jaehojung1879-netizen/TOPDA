Surcharge waiver — transitional rule

Even if the transfer date falls after 9 May 2026, the waiver still applies when the sale contract was signed and the down payment received by that date and the transfer happens within 4 months of the contract (6 months in newly designated regulated areas). A closing date of 10 May or later does not automatically trigger the surcharge.
