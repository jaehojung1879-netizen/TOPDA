Balance-Day Settlement Calculator

Management fee proration · prepaid fee · long-term repair reserve

① Prorated management fee

The bill for the month right before closing day.
From day 1 through closing day.

② Prepaid fee & long-term repair reserve

Deposit the seller paid at move-in; refunded by buyer to seller.
If a tenant paid this monthly, it is refunded landlord → tenant at lease end.
