How the total score is calculated

Each factor is scored 0–100, then combined as a weighted average for the total (0–100). Factors with no data or no input are dropped and their weight is redistributed to the rest.

FactorWeightScore formula (0–100)
Upside23%Trend of recent price per ㎡ (linear-regression slope → annualized) at 65%, plus Jeonse ratio (lower = pricier area) at 35%. +20%/yr = full marks, 0% = 50.
Transit19%Walking distance to the nearest subway station. ≤300m = 100, ≥1.5km = 0 (linear).
School19%Distance to the nearest elementary school. ≤150m = 100, ≥1.2km = 0 (linear).
Price stability14%Lower coefficient of variation (std ÷ mean) of recent prices scores higher. Fewer than 2 samples = neutral (60).
Scale10%≥2,000 households = 100, ≤300 = 20 (linear).
Age10%Built within 2 years = 100, ≥35 years = 10 (linear).
Liquidity5%Deals in the last 6 months. ≥15 = 100, 0 = 30.
Budget fit25%
when budget entered		Among units at or below budget, closer to your budget scores higher. Entering a budget adds this factor with the largest weight.

※ The price trend uses monthly average price per ㎡ to remove distortion from which unit sizes traded. When a complex has no per-complex Jeonse ratio, the regional average is used and marked "(area)" on the card. This score is a reference indicator from public transaction/open data and does not guarantee returns.
