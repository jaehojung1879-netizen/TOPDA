Items that are often left out of the estimate (additional costs)

Even if the initial estimate seems low, if the items below are missing, it will increase to ‘additional work’ during construction. Before contract Please specify inclusions/exclusions.

VAT (10%)
There are many estimates ‘excluding VAT’ — be sure to include it when comparing the total amount.
Demolition and waste disposal
Ladder truck, gunny sack, and waste transportation costs. The more built it is, the bigger it is.
Replacing old equipment
Items that are ‘added when opened’ such as plumbing, electric circuit breaker, hot water distributor, etc.
Leveling and plastering
Floor level difference correction (self-leveling), wall cleaning
Move-in cleaning, grout, elastic coat
Easy to miss out on as a separate cost for the closing stage
Elevator/Management Room
Construction deposit, elevator maintenance, and management office reporting costs

Real ways to reduce your budget

  • Rating adjustment — Lower the material grade of ‘big items’ such as kitchen, bathroom, and windows (same cost savings are significant).
  • Keep the place clean — Even if you just replace the wallpaper and lighting while leaving the floor and windows in good condition, the atmosphere will change significantly.
  • Comparison of 3 to 5 companies — To compare, you must receive a quote with the same specifications. How to compare quotes.
  • Take advantage of off-season — If you avoid the peak seasons of spring and fall, when moving and interior design activities are concentrated, you will have more room in schedule and unit price.
  • Agreement on additional construction consent procedures — The contract specifies that ‘no additional construction costs can be charged without written consent.’
