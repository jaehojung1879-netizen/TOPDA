CHAPTER 4

Checklist when replacing windows

There is a lot of advance preparation for replacement, including exterior changes, noise, and actual measurements.

  • Report to the management office — Prior consent from the union and management office when changing the exterior
  • understanding with neighbors — Safety net, noise, entry/exit schedule
  • actual dimensions — Be sure to conduct on-site inspection before estimating
  • window series — Even for the same brand, each series has different ratings.
  • insect screen — Fine insect screen vs general. Check for inclusion
  • exterior finish — Whether external wall reinforcement or silicone finish is included
  • defect warranty — 5 years for windows and construction (Framework Act on the Construction Industry)

CHAPTER 9

Frequently Asked Questions

Are system windows necessarily better than regular double-glazed windows?

System windows have excellent insulation and airtightness, and there is almost no condensation. However, the unit price is high and it relies heavily on repair parts. Depending on the budget and household conditions (floor, view, noise), standard double-glazed windows grade 1 are often sufficient, so compare based on the grade (Uw).

What is thermal transmittance (Uw)?

This value indicates the degree to which heat escapes through a window. The lower the value, the better the insulation. For exterior windows, energy efficiency level 1 (approximately Uw 0.8~1.2) is recommended. In order to compare, prices must be written in numbers on the quote.

Even though I changed the window, condensation continues.

Most condensation is caused by lack of ventilation. Even if you change to system windows, if you do not ventilate briefly 2-3 times a day, indoor moisture may not escape and condensation may form again.

How do I check if it is genuine?

Check with the engraving/hologram on the frame. To avoid using non-genuine profiles, we recommend that you request photos of the engraving after installation.
