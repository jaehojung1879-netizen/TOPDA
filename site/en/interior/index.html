Interior renovation, one topic at a time

A plain-language overview of finishes — flooring, wallpaper, tile, bathroom, kitchen, windows — plus a contractor guide covering firm types, quotes, contracts and defect repair. Written as practical criteria for choosing and reviewing quotes yourself, not as advertising.
