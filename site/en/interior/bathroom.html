CHAPTER 2

process sequence

Each process requires drying and curing time. Since waterproofing and gradients cannot be seen once they are covered, please take a photo record.

  1. demolition — Tile, pottery, faucet, ceiling (1 day)
  2. Plumbing/Electricity — Change of faucet location, ventilation/outlet (1 day)
  3. waterproof — Apply 2 times, dry for 24 hours each — Most important
  4. Plastering and drafting — Floor drain slope (1~2%)
  5. tile — Wall → floor order (1-2 days)
  6. Joint and caulking — Corner silicone closure
  7. Pottery and Faucets — Toilet, sink, bathtub, shower (half day)
  8. Ceiling/ventilator — SMC ceiling + exhaust fan (half day)
  9. Inspection/cleaning — Leak testing (24 hours fresh water), cleaning
CHAPTER 3

Waterproofing — the single most important thing

Poor waterproofing leads to water leaks in the lower floors and becomes the number one cause of disputes between construction companies.

⚠
Apply 2 times + Observe drying time + Reinforce cornersThis is the standard. After waterproofing, check the downstairs ceiling with a fresh water (fill water) test.
waterproof type
Urethane coating / cement based / sheet. The block-type diet is the most common
thickness
Apply twice, total thickness 1.5mm or more
rising part
Apply up to 20~30cm on the wall (especially around the bathtub)
drying time
More than 24 hours. Construction is prohibited on rainy days and when humidity is above 80%.
water leak test
After waterproofing, fill the floor with water for 24 hours → Check the ceiling downstairs.
CHAPTER 4

Ventilation — the key to mold prevention

Ventilator air volume and duct path determine mold growth.

  • Ventilator air volume — 60~80 m³/h based on 1.5 pyeong. If it is weak, it goes straight to mold.
  • With or without windows — Even if there is a window, an exhaust fan is essential (winter ventilation).
  • duct length — The longer and more heeled the less airflow. Preferably straight.
  • Timer/humidity sensor — The automatic driving model is easy to manage.

CHAPTER 9

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if it is properly waterproofed?

After waterproofing, check the downstairs ceiling with a fresh water (leakage) test by filling the floor with water and leaving it for 24 hours. Specifying freshwater testing as the contractor's responsibility in the contract will help prevent disputes.

I have a window. Do I really need an exhaust fan?

You need it. For ventilation in winter, when it is difficult to open windows, an exhaust fan is essential even if there is a window. The air volume of 60~80 m³/h based on 1.5 pyeong and the short duct path reduce mold.

What tile should I use on my bathroom floor?

Porcelain (porcelain) non-slip tiles with slip resistance R10 to R11 are recommended. Glossy polish is slippery when wet and is therefore unsuitable for bathroom floors.

The grout quickly discolored.

Regular white cement joints may discolor within 1 to 2 years. Contamination will be reduced if you replace it with epoxy joints that are resistant to waterproofing and mold.
