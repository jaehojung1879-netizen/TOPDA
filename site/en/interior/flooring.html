CHAPTER 2

Thickness, heat, noise

‘Thermal conductivity’ is important in apartment floor heating. The thinner it is, the more efficient it is at generating heat, but there is a trade-off in making your footsteps louder.

Living room and bedroom standards 7~8mm It's a stand. Heat and noise are well balanced. Thin LVT is suitable for kitchens and laundry rooms exposed to water, and thick wood is suitable for living rooms where texture is important.

CHAPTER 3

How to choose

Candidates are narrowed down by factoring in four factors: family composition, space use, management tendencies, and budget.

It fits well in times like this.

  • I want to use it comfortably for a long time → Kangmaru
  • Save your budget or rent → Laminate flooring/flooring
  • It is a space frequently exposed to water → LVT
  • Texture and depth are the top priority → solid wood flooring

In times like this, think again

  • Pets/Children → Laminated floors are checked for scratches and noise.
  • House with large humidity changes → Be careful of joints opening on hardwood floors
  • Underfloor heating gives priority to heat generation → Thick wood is efficient ↓
  • Frequent water cleaning habits → Hardwood floors and hardwood are vulnerable to flooding

Estimate/construction check

If you look at the unit price per pyeong, removal, skirting boards, and thresholds are omitted and added later. Nail the scope of inclusion before signing the contract.

  • Brand, product number — Specify the manufacturer and series name
  • Demolition and leveling work — Included or not, unit price indicated separately
  • Baseboard, threshold — Inclusion/exclusion statement
  • redundancy — Request to keep 1 box for repair (in preparation for discontinuation of product)
  • defect warranty — Maru is usually 2 years
CHAPTER 6

Defects and Management

Most flooring defects result from poor bottom flatness, moisture, and adhesion. If you know the cause, you can distinguish between construction defects and careless use.

  • Heaving/creaking — Poor flatness and adhesion of the lower part. Partial re-construction.
  • joint opening — Changes in humidity. Re-construction if it occurs within 1-2 months.
  • discoloration — Prolonged exposure to direct sunlight. It is not a construction defect.
  • water marks — Gangmaru is permanently deformed when submerged for more than 1 hour.

CHAPTER 9

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between reinforced floor and reinforced floor?

The surface finish and construction method are different. Hardwood flooring is constructed by gluing veneer together, and laminate flooring is clicked into place with a laminate surface. Laminated flooring has a good heat and noise balance, and laminate flooring is cheaper and faster to install, but the sound of footsteps is louder.

Is thinner always better for underfloor heating?

If you look at heat generation efficiency, thinner is more advantageous, but the thinner it is, the louder the sound of footsteps becomes. For living rooms and bedrooms, 7~8mm is a good balance between heat generation and noise.

This is a house that frequently spills water. Will Gangmaru be okay?

River ridges may become permanently deformed if submerged for more than one hour. We recommend partial application of LVT, which is highly waterproof, in places that frequently come into contact with water, such as the kitchen, laundry room, and entrance.

How far should I go in terms of eco-friendliness?

As well as the grade of the flooring material (E0 or higher recommended). Adhesive used in constructionAlso check the rating. Harmful substances from adhesives can affect indoor air quality.
