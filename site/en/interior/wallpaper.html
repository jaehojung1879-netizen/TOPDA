Construction sequence

  1. Putty and leveling work — Filling defects after removing existing wallpaper
  2. Primer application — Glue absorption and mold prevention
  3. Pasting/cutting — Differences in grass concentration and feeding time
  4. papering — Butting (silk) or overlapping (lamination)
  5. Dry 24-48 hours — If it is humid, it can become moldy and flaky.

1~2 days based on 25 pyeong. +1 day for old houses with a lot of putty.

CHAPTER 3

How to choose

Decide between silk and laminated paper based on your period of residence, the nature of the space, and your cleaning habits.

If silk fits well

  • Durability is important because the house will be lived for a long time.
  • These are walls that come into contact with hands and contamination, such as in the kitchen and living room.
  • I want a clean finish without seams.

When the lamination and painting match well

  • The goal is a bright and smooth closing before leasing or selling.
  • Emphasis on breathability (eco-friendly paper)
  • Painting: I want smooth walls without seams.

CHAPTER 8

Frequently Asked Questions

Silk or laminated paper, which is better for a long life?

If you live there for a long time, silk is advantageous. It can be cleaned with a wet mop and has a longer durability of 10 to 15 years than laminated paper, which lasts 5 to 7 years. On the other hand, if you are renting or living short-term or breathability is important, lamination is reasonable.

There is mold on the wallpaper. Is this a defect?

Mold is usually caused by condensation or water leaks. If it is due to poor construction (dryness or lack of ventilation) within one year of moving in, it is grounds for re-construction, but if it is an insulation or ventilation problem, the cause must be addressed first to prevent recurrence.

It's the same wallpaper, but the color looks different on each wall.

Even if the product number is the same, the color may differ if the production cycle (lot) is different. Therefore, the principle is to order a sufficient quantity in the same lot at one time.

Would painting be better instead of wallpaper?

Painting has no seams and is easy to repair, but putty and leveling work are more important as wall defects are exposed. If the wall is in good condition and you want a smooth finish, painting may be advantageous. If there are many defects, wallpaper may be advantageous.
