CHAPTER 8

Frequently Asked Questions

Which should you choose between artificial marble and engineered stone?

If smooth and repairable joints are important, artificial marble is advantageous, and if durability against heat and scratches is important, engineered stone (quartz) is advantageous. However, quartz has visible seams and is difficult to repair.

What do I need to check to turn on the induction?

Induction requires a dedicated 220V circuit. A 30A breaker is recommended for the 3-hole 8kW class. If a dishwasher or oven is added, expansion of the distribution board may be necessary, so check the electric capacity at the layout drawing stage.

How do you compare a brand kitchen and a home-made kitchen?

You should not just look at the price; you should match the specifications of the top, door, hardware, and body to be the same and then compare them. Instead of being able to specify materials directly, the priest must explicitly request hardware and countertop specifications.

Can I remove the top seam?

Artificial marble can be processed without joints, but there are length restrictions (usually around 3.6m). Anything longer than that will cause seams, so check at the placement stage.
