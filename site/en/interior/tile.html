Rice cake construction vs. bond construction

Rice cake construction (pressing)

Floor/slope adjustment

Construction is done by placing mortar one by one like a rice cake. Bathroom floors are almost standard as they are advantageous for floor flatness and slope adjustment.

Construction cost: Around 50,000 to 70,000 won per ㎡

Bond construction (adhesion)

Wall, quick construction

Attaches directly to a flat surface coated with dedicated adhesive. It's quick and clean, but the bottom must be flat.

Construction cost: Around 30,000 to 50,000 won per ㎡

Half of a tile's finish is grout. Both finishing during construction and maintenance during occupancy are important.

CHAPTER 5

Frequently occurring defects

Most tile defects result from lack of adhesion, poor gradient, and insufficient waterproofing.

  • Lifting and peeling — Lack of mortar during rice cake construction. If you knock and it makes a hollow sound, reinstall.
  • joint cracking — Shrinkage after construction. Repair with caulking.
  • poor draft — Water pooling on bathroom floor. Construction defect, reason for re-construction.
  • poor waterproofing — Water leak in bathroom downstairs. It is essential to check the waterproofing construction stage.

CHAPTER 8

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use polished tiles on the bathroom floor?

Not recommended. Glossy polish is slippery when wet and is not suitable for bathroom floors. For bathroom floors, use porcelain with slip resistance of R10 to R11.

Which is better: rice cake construction or bond construction?

They have different uses. For bathroom floors that need to maintain a gradient (slope of water draining), rice cake construction is the standard, and for flat walls, quick and clean bond construction is advantageous.

What is the best grout color?

White is clean, but dirt shows up quickly. Light gray is acceptable, and for frequently contaminated areas such as bathrooms and kitchens, consider epoxy grout, which is resistant to waterproofing and mold.

It's the same tile, but the color is slightly different.

Tiles have color differences depending on the production lot. Therefore, the principle is to secure the same lot at once, including surplus.
