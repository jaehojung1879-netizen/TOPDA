Interior Standard Contract & Payment Splitting

Last updated·6 min read
one line conclusion

The Fair Trade Commission’s standard contract + ‘30% down payment / 40% mid-payment / 30% balance payment’ installment payment is the basic formula for preventing interior disputes.

Why you should use a standard contract

Fair Trade Commission ‘Interior Architecture and Window Construction Standard Contract’explicitly includes a clause protecting the lessee (owner), which serves as a decisive basis in the event of a dispute. If the construction company only uses its own form, there is a high risk that the tenant will agree to unfavorable provisions (exemption liability, exclusive agreement, etc.).

Key provisions of standard contract

  • scope of construction: Materials, specifications, and quantities are specified in the attached quotation.
  • construction period: Start date, completion date + liquidated damages (0.3‰ of construction cost per day)
  • payment: Split ratio and timing
  • Defect repair warranty period: Differential by process (1 year for wallpapering and painting, 2 years for flooring, tiles and windows, 3 years for waterproofing)
  • Additional construction: Mandatory written consent procedure
  • Dispute Resolution: Priority application to the Korea Consumer Agency and Construction Dispute Mediation Committee

Splitting the payment — ‘30 / 40 / 30’ principle

divisionratioWhen to pay
down payment30%Upon conclusion of the contract
interim payment40%Upon completion of demolition and woodworking (at 50% process progress)
balance30%After completion inspection + repair of defects

Requests for down payment of more than 50% are immediately rejected.. If the construction company goes bankrupt or disappears, recovery is almost impossible.

Performance bond — Required for construction worth over 15 million won

If you receive a ‘performance bond’ up to 10% of the construction cost, even if the construction company disappears or goes bankrupt midway, you can entrust it to another company to complete the project. Construction Mutual Aid Association·SGI Seoul GuaranteeIssued by.
