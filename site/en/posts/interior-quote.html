The standard for comparing interior design estimates

Last updated·9 minute read
one line conclusion

Only quotations separated into the four types of ‘material name, specifications, quantity, and unit price’ can be compared. The price per pyeong/sik is the start of a dispute.

4 conditions for a good quotation

  1. Material name — Manufacturer/model name (e.g. LX Z:IN Gangmaru ECO 200, KCC painted eco-friendly paint)
  2. standard — Thickness, color, grade
  3. quantity — ㎡, EA unit (total amount + unit)
  4. unit price — Material cost + construction cost separated

Bad Quote Pattern – Immediate Rejection

  • ‘Japanese food OOO 10,000 won’ format (no details)
  • Only indicate ‘OOO Won per pyeong’ (no distinction between materials and construction)
  • The proportion of ‘Other miscellaneous work OOO Won’ is more than 30% (opaque)
  • Manufacturer/model name not stated (‘high-quality wallpaper’ type)
  • Unclear whether VAT (value-added tax) is included

Comparison of 3 to 5 companies — under the same conditions

Request a quote from each company using the same materials, scope, and options. Only then will price comparisons be meaningful. When requesting a quote, provide the following sheet:

  • Wallpaper: Silk/Paper Separation + Area
  • Flooring: Hardwood/hardwood/flooring + area + manufacturer
  • Tiles: 2 bathrooms + kitchen + entrance area
  • Kitchen furniture: length of upper cabinet + length of lower cabinet + top material (engineered stone, ceramic)
  • Bathroom: Toilet, sink, shower booth manufacturer (Daelim/American Standard, etc.)
  • Sash: System window/General + Insulation grade
  • Painting/painting + system air conditioner location

Additional construction – codification of consent procedures

Additional construction often occurs due to water leaks or wall cracks discovered during demolition. Include the following clause in your quote or contract:

i
‘When additional construction occurs, the contractor notifies the lessee (contractor) in writing (including a messenger) of photos, estimates, and estimated construction period, and proceeds only after the lessee’s written consent. ‘The cost of additional construction carried out without consent cannot be claimed.’
