8 ways to choose an interior design company

Last updated·11 min read
one line conclusion

Narrow down the candidates to only those companies that meet the four requirements: ‘Construction business registration + standard contract + defect repair warranty + 5 or more construction cases’. Then compare quotes, reviews, and communication.

01. Construction business registration status — KISCON inquiry

Registration is mandatory for ‘interior construction projects’ above a certain scale. Construction Industry Knowledge Information System (KISCON)You can check the registration status, construction ability evaluation amount, and defect history using the business registration number. Small-scale partial construction is possible even if it is not registered, but If the total construction cost is more than 15 million won, registered companyIt's safe.

02. Construction performance/portfolio — ‘On-site photos’ are the key

Than the website and Instagram photos ‘Site occupied for more than 6 months after completion’Ask to see it in person. Anyone who has just completed construction is pretty. The true measure of skill is the peeling of the wallpaper, silicone mold, and cracks in the finish after 6 months of residence. After obtaining consent, it is best to visit one or two places in person.

03. Fair Trade Commission ‘Standard contract for interior architecture and window construction’

The standard contract specifies the defect repair warranty period for each process (1 year for wallpapering and painting, 2 years for flooring, tiles and windows, 3 years for waterproofing), 0.3‰ of the construction cost per day as liquidated damages, and procedures for consenting to additional construction. Fair Trade Commission Standard ContractDownload and compare. In places where the company only uses its own form, the tenant is at a great disadvantage in the event of a dispute.

04. Defect repair guarantee insurance — performance guarantee bond

If you receive a ‘performance bond’ up to 10% of the construction cost, you can entrust the completion to another company even if the construction company goes bankrupt or disappears. Construction Mutual Aid Association·SGI Seoul GuaranteePublished by . Be sure to request for construction exceeding 15 million won.

05. Payment division — ‘Advance payment 30 / Advance payment 40 / Balance payment 30’

Requests for a down payment of more than 50% are rejected. The balance is After completion inspection Payment. Payment in installments based on process progress is standard. The most certain way to minimize losses in the event of bankruptcy or disappearance of a construction company.

06. Compare quotes — Avoid ‘Japanese cuisine/per pyeong’ quotes

The following four items must all be listed separately in the quotation:

  • Material name (Manufacturer/Model — Example: LX Z:IN Kangmaru ECO 200)
  • standard (㎡, thickness, color, etc.)
  • quantity (㎡, EA)
  • unit price + Construction costseparate

‘Estimate for Japanese food (OOO Won for a whole meal)’ or ‘OOO Won per pyeong’ is the start of a dispute. The detailed comparison method is The standard for comparing interior design estimates reference.

07. Communication — KakaoTalk/Messenger + Photos every day

Promise to receive on-site photos and progress via KakaoTalk or messenger every day. The standard is ‘1 sheet per day’. It serves as the strongest evidence in the event of a dispute. The fastest way is to create a group chat room using the site manager's cell phone.

08. Reviews – Local communities are more accurate

Interior cafe/review sites are a mixture of advertisements and self-made reviews. Take advantage of:

  • Cafe, Mom Cafe, Carrot Neighborhood for residents of the complex
  • Today's house Construction review (high ratio of real names and actual photos)
  • Korea Consumer Agency Dispute history inquiry (1372)

Large corporations like Hanssem and LX vs. local companies – which is better?

Large corporations (Hanssem, LX, Lee Kun, Hyundai L&C)
Standardized construction, materials, and warranty. The price is 20-40% more expensive than the neighborhood. Stable after-sales service and defect repair. The package product is well structured.
Local interior design company
Price competitiveness + material freedom + design customization. However, it is necessary to check the reliability and risk of bankruptcy of the construction company. Companies that have been in the same position for more than 5 years are relatively safe.
One-person CEO (directly managed)
It is the cheapest, but has limitations in terms of warranty, documentation, and dispute response. Suitable for partial construction (wallpaper, floor only). Full remodeling is not recommended.

In case of dispute — free mediation window

  • Korea Consumer Agency (1372) — Free consultation and mediation of interior disputes
  • Construction Dispute Mediation Committee — Defect/payment dispute resolution
  • Competent local government (building department of district office) — Report of unregistered construction
