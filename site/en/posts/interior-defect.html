Response to defect repair claims and disputes

Last updated·7 min read
one line conclusion

The standard is 1 year for wallpapering and painting, 2 years for flooring, tiles and windows, and 3 years for waterproofing. Immediately upon discovery, notify via photo or messenger → 1st contractor → If not, Korea Consumer Agency (1372).

Standard defect repair period for each process

processmaintenance period
Wallpaper and painting1 year
Flooring, tiles, windows, kitchen furniture2 years
Waterproofing work3 years
Architectural structure and equipment construction2~10 years (Appendix 4 of the Framework Act on Construction Industry)

In principle, defects discovered within this period will be repaired free of charge. Even if it is not specified in the contract, it is protected by the Fair Trade Commission's standard contract and the Construction Industry Framework Act.

Defect discovery → Claim procedure

  1. Photos and videos immediately after discovery — With date display + measurer
  2. Notify the construction company in writing (KakaoTalk/Messenger) — Keep records of responses
  3. 1st repair request — Usually visit within 2 weeks
  4. Non-compliance with 2nd remuneration — Application for mediation by Korea Consumer Agency and Construction Dispute Mediation Committee
  5. coordination breakdown — Civil litigation (small claims trial)

Free dispute resolution window

  • Korea Consumer Agency (1372) — Free consultation and mediation of interior disputes. Average turnaround time is 30 days. There is no enforcement, but the agreement rate is high.
  • Construction Dispute Mediation Committee — Resolution of defect/payment disputes. Includes free attorney advice.
  • Korea Legal Aid Corporation (132) — Free legal consultation

Receive compensation through performance bond

If you have signed up for a performance bond, if the construction company refuses to pay, the surety company (Construction Mutual Aid Association·SGI), ‘defect repair claims’ are possible. The warranty company entrusts the repairs to another company.
