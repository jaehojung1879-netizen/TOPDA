Move-in Registration, Mail, and Utility Changes

Updated July 2026·7 min read
In brief

Complete the legally required address process promptly. Tenants should handle the Korean protection steps that apply to them on move-in day and should not assume every service transfers automatically.

On this page

  1. Address registration
  2. Government24 related services
  3. Services to transfer directly
  4. Move-in day record

Address registration

The Korean source guide states that domestic move-in registration is required within 14 days and that tenants should act on moving day because address registration is part of establishing protection against third parties. Korean citizens can use Government24 or the local community service center. Foreign residents should update the address through the immigration process applicable to their status and confirm the protection steps for their lease.

  • Bring the identification and authorization documents required for the filer.
  • For a lease, ask whether the fixed-date stamp can be completed at the same time.
  • Keep confirmation receipts with the signed lease.

Government24 related services

  • Mail forwarding: Korea Post can forward mail for the available service period.
  • Utility support: some address-linked services may be offered during the move-in filing.
  • Vehicle address: check whether the relevant vehicle record updates automatically.
  • School administration: primary-school and other placement processes can differ.
  • Civil-defense or reserve records: domestic residents should confirm automatic updates where applicable.

Move-in day record

  • Photograph utility meters and the condition of every room.
  • Confirm keys, access cards, parking registration, and building rules.
  • Notify building management and reserve any elevator or move-in access.
  • Save the handover record, meter photographs, lease, address filings, and utility confirmations together.
