Privacy Policy

This page describes what TOPDA actually does, based on the code that is running on the site. Every tool listed below is live today — none of it is a future plan.

TOPDA has no accounts and no sign-up, and calculator inputs are never sent to a server. Analytics and advertising are active, and board posts and comments are stored on a server. Everything below reflects code that is actually running on the site.

1. Tools in use and what they process

ToolWhat it processes

Supabase Stores board posts and comments, since they must be visible to every visitor: title, body, nickname, timestamp, private flag, and a random owner token that lets you edit or delete your own post. No IP address or email is collected or stored.

There is no login and no sign-up, and no form asks for your name, national ID, phone number, or email. Anything you type into the board is stored as part of that post, so please do not post personal or sensitive information.

2. Third parties and international transfers

TOPDA does not sell personal data or pass it to third parties for advertising. Because of the tools above, access data and cookies are shared with Google LLC (Analytics, AdSense), NAVER Corp., Supabase Inc. (board storage), GitHub, Inc. (hosting) and jsDelivr (web fonts), and may be transferred to and processed on servers outside Korea. Each provider's server location and retention period follow that provider's policy and project settings.

3. Refusing cookies and ads

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Calculators and checklists keep working without them.
  • Personalised ads only — Google Ad Settings, aboutads.info
  • Analytics only — Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on
  • Google's practices — Advertising and Data policies

4. Retention, deletion, and your rights

  • Posts and comments are kept until you or the operator delete them. There is no automatic purge.
  • Browser-stored values can be cleared entirely by you at any time.
  • Analytics and ad cookies follow each service's retention policy and cookie expiry.
  • Under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act you may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing.
  • TOPDA is not directed at children under 14.

5. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests go through the TOPDA board (KO). No sign-up is required, and posting privately keeps the body visible only to you and the operator. When requesting a deletion, include the post title and roughly when it was written.
