Privacy Policy

This page describes what TOPDA actually does, based on the code that is running on the site. Every tool listed below is live today — none of it is a future plan.

i
Summary TOPDA has no accounts and no sign-up, and calculator inputs are never sent to a server. However, analytics (Google Analytics 4, Naver Analytics) and advertising (Google AdSense) are active, and the message board and comments are stored in Supabase so other visitors can read them. Calculator and checklist values stay in your browser's localStorage.

1. What we process

  • Device and access data — browser and device type, approximate region, referrer, pages visited, time on page. Collected automatically by the analytics tools.
  • Cookies and advertising identifiers — for analytics and for serving and measuring ads.
  • Text you write yourself — the title, body, and nickname you type into the board or comments.
  • Owner token — a random UUID that lets you edit or delete your own post later. It is not linked to your name or contact details.
  • Browser-local values — calculator inputs, checklist state, chosen language. Never sent to a server.
  • Usage events — calculator runs, share-button clicks, outbound link clicks, checklist ticks. These record which tool was used, not the amounts you entered.

TOPDA has no login and no sign-up, and no form asks for your name, national ID, phone number, or email. If you type contact details into a board nickname or body field, they are stored as part of that post — so please do not post personal or sensitive information.

2. Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-7NE57E1KLH) on every page via /assets/analytics.js, with anonymize_ip: true enabled. Custom events include calculator_run, calc_complete, share_click, outbound_click, checklist_check, apt_page_view, apt_calculator_click, and guide_click. Event parameters record the calculator name and entry path only — never the figures you entered.

See how Google uses data from partner sites, and opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Naver Analytics

For Korean search traffic analysis we use Naver Analytics (account ID 25db1b1d375e68), loading wcs.naver.net/wcslog.js on every page to record referrers and page views. Collection and opt-out follow Naver's own policies.

4. Google AdSense, cookies, and ad identifiers

TOPDA serves ads through Google AdSense (ca-pub-6725359021570843). Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies and advertising identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

  • Ad scripts run only on finished content and calculator pages. This privacy policy, the contact, editorial-policy and corrections pages, the board and comments, internal search results, pages excluded from search indexing, and error pages carry no ad script.
  • Turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings, or opt out across participating vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Google's advertising data practices are covered by its Advertising and Data policies.

5. Supabase

Board posts and comments must be visible to every visitor, so those two features — and only those — are stored in Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL service. Calculator and checklist values are never sent to Supabase.

  • Tables — board_posts and content_comments. Public reads go through views (board_posts_public, content_comments_public) that exclude private-post bodies and owner tokens.
  • Fields stored — category, title, body, nickname (blank shows as "anonymous"), created and updated timestamps, private flag, owner token (UUID).
  • Access control — the key exposed in the browser is a public anon key; actual read and write permissions are enforced by Supabase row-level security policies.
  • If Supabase is not configured, posts stay only in the localStorage of the browser that wrote them.

The Supabase project's data region and backup retention period follow that service's project settings, and Google Analytics data retention follows the setting in the GA4 dashboard. TOPDA does not assert specific countries or durations of its own — those services' policies and settings govern.

7. Board and comments

  • Public posts — title, body, nickname, and timestamp are visible to everyone.
  • Private posts — the title appears in the list with a lock icon, but only a browser holding the owner token, and the operator, can open the body.
  • Owner token — generated randomly when you post and stored in your browser. If you clear browser data the token is gone, and you will need to ask the operator to delete that post.
  • IP addresses and email — TOPDA's code does not collect or store either.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Posts and comments — kept until you or the operator delete them. There is no automatic purge schedule.
  • localStorage values — until you clear browser data or use a tool's reset button. The operator cannot access them.
  • Analytics — governed by each provider's retention policy and dashboard settings (see section 5).
  • Ad cookies — until each cookie expires or you delete it.

10. Refusing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Calculators and checklists keep working without cookies.

  • Chrome — Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies
  • Safari — Settings › Privacy › Block all cookies
  • Edge — Settings › Cookies and site permissions
  • Personalised ads only — Google Ad Settings
  • Analytics only — Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on

11. Your rights

  • Delete a post you wrote — use the delete control in the browser you wrote it from, or ask through the contact channel with the post title and approximate time.
  • Delete browser-stored values — clear site data in your browser at any time.
  • Refuse analytics and ad collection — see section 10.
  • Under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act you may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing.
  • TOPDA is not directed at children under 14 and has no guardian-consent flow.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests are received through the TOPDA board (KO). No sign-up is required, and posting privately keeps the body visible only to you and the operator. When requesting a deletion, include the post title and roughly when it was written so it can be identified. TOPDA does not operate a separate email intake — the board is the contact channel.

13. Changes

If this policy changes because of new features or legal requirements, the updated version and its effective date are published on this page. New measurement, advertising, or storage tools are documented here at the time they are introduced.
