3. Data sources and their limits

Transaction prices, household counts, official assessed values, and price indices come from public data published by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and the Korea Real Estate Board. The site presents that data in processed form and does not warrant its accuracy or completeness.

Fields absent from the public data are left blank rather than estimated. When a collection run fails, the previous value is kept and gaps are not backfilled with recent figures. Complexes with few recorded transactions are labelled as such.
